# ASSET_RULES.md

Target Style:
Low poly arcade visuals

Asset Sources:
Many future assets will come from Kenney assets.

Preferred Formats:
- GLB preferred
- OBJ + MTL acceptable
- PNG for UI
- OGG for compressed audio

Optimization Rules:
- Keep polygon counts reasonable
- Prioritize readability over detail
- Reuse materials when possible
- Avoid oversized textures
- Minimize transparency effects
- Maintain mobile-friendly performance

Visual Philosophy:
Gameplay readability is more important than realism.
