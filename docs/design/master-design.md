# Zombie Vehicle Survival
## Master Game Design Document
### Arcade Junkyard Zombie Survival
### Internal Studio Design Reference

# High Concept

Zombie Vehicle Survival is a fast paced endless zombie destruction sandbox focused on:
- momentum
- chaos
- compulsive replayability
- satisfying vehicle physics
- escalating destruction
- persistent progression
- arcade style survival

The player controls a single vehicle in an endless sandbox environment and attempts to survive as long as possible while killing massive numbers of zombies using:
- ramming
- driving skill
- optional weapon systems
- momentum
- build crafting
- progression upgrades

The game is NOT:
- a racing simulator
- a military simulator
- a grim survival horror game
- a photorealistic apocalypse game

The game IS:
- chaotic
- stylish
- aggressive
- funny
- destructive
- replayable
- arcade focused
- momentum driven

The intended emotional experience is:
“This apocalypse is insane and awesome.”

The target identity is:
## “Mad Max meets Zombieland”

# Core Gameplay Loop

The player enters an endless sandbox environment with a selected vehicle.

The objective is simple:
Survive as long as possible and kill as many zombies as possible before the vehicle is destroyed.

During a run the player:
- drives through zombie hordes
- builds momentum
- creates streaks
- unlocks temporary upgrades
- survives escalating waves
- experiments with builds
- chases higher kill counts

Eventually:
- the vehicle becomes overwhelmed
- the player dies
- progression rewards are granted

The player then returns to the garage to:
- upgrade stats
- unlock new vehicles
- unlock weapons
- improve builds
- pursue mastery progression
- start another run

# Core Gameplay Identity

This is:
Momentum Addiction Design

The player should constantly feel:
- “I’m getting stronger.”

- “This run feels better.”
- “One more run.”

# Vehicle Physics Philosophy

The game should use simcade style physics:
- believable
- weighty
- momentum based
- traction based
- forgiving
- fun

Vehicles should NEVER feel:
- twitchy
- weightless
- floaty
- instant

# Steering Philosophy

The left thumbstick controls steering angle.

At low speeds:
- responsive steering
- tight turning

At high speeds:
- reduced responsiveness
- wider turns
- less grip
- instability

# Gas and Brake Philosophy

Gas pedal:
- applies engine torque
- creates acceleration force

Brake system:
- brake slows the car while moving forward
- holding brake near stop enters reverse
- reverse uses brake as throttle

# Momentum and Grip

Grip changes dynamically.

Low Slip:
- full traction

Medium Slip:
- controlled drift

High Slip:
- loss of control

# Camera Philosophy

The camera should:
- lag slightly during acceleration
- compress during braking
- sway during turning
- yaw during drifting

# Vehicle Damage System

The starter vehicle survives roughly:
10 direct zombie impacts.

Vehicle damage escalates visually:
- scratches
- dents
- sparks
- smoke
- bent panels
- dragging parts
- fire

# CRITICAL ZOMBIE RULE

Zombies ONLY die if physically killed.

Valid kills:
- direct vehicle impact
- crushing
- slicing
- explosions
- weapon hits

If zombies merely touch the vehicle:
- they DO NOT die
- they DO NOT count as kills

Instead:
- they shove the vehicle
- slam into doors
- scrape against panels
- damage the car over time

If the player stalls:
the zombies eventually destroy the vehicle.
